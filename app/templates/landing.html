The platform

What this is

A family office runs on a small team and a large number of moving parts: brokerage accounts at several custodians, private funds that call capital on their own schedules, properties with lenders and covenants, entities that lend to each other, a stream of statements and notices by email, and a foundation that writes checks every month. Most offices hold this together with spreadsheets and memory.

This platform replaces the spreadsheets with ten focused apps on one PostgreSQL database. A property added in Real Estate shows up in the Family Office balance sheet; a distribution recorded once flows to performance, cash and the investor portal; a document filed from the inbox is linked to the deal it belongs to. Every write is permission-checked and audited.

It is a Flask application, server-rendered, with HTMX for in-place updates. It runs in one container next to Postgres and deploys anywhere that runs Docker.
